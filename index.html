MVP scope and design reasoning

Must-have in MVP

Search by service + city, category tiles, filter panel, provider cards, provider profile (services, fees range, doctors, photos, timings, map, call/WhatsApp), emergency shortcut, free “List your business” and “Submit a correction” flows. Booking and payments come later: first win trust through accurate data.

Provider card shows

Name, type, distance, open-now status, species treated, 24×7 badge, verified badge, starting consultation fee, rating, and two actions: Call and Directions. Everything needed to decide without opening the profile.

Mobile-first choices

One-thumb layout: search and Call buttons in the lower half, filters in a bottom sheet, a floating Emergency button that filters to ambulances and 24×7 hospitals. Large tap targets, high contrast, works on slow networks.

Trust and growth loop

Verified badge only after a phone or document check. “Submit a Correction” keeps data fresh; “List Your Business” feeds supply. Hindi and English toggle for tier-2 cities.

The repeatable workflow

1. Idea inFounder types one line into a Google Form or Notion page.
2. Researchn8n sends it to Claude with web search: 5 facts and sources.
3. CopyClaude writes 3 hook options and slide text (max 25 words/slide) in the brand voice.
4. DesignCopy is pushed to a Canva brand template via Bulk Create; colours and fonts are locked.
5. ReviewSlack/WhatsApp alert with the Canva link. Founder edits text, approves.
6. PublishExport PNG/PDF; Buffer schedules to Instagram and LinkedIn.
Why this holds up: design stays consistent because only text changes inside a locked template. A fact-check step (sources saved in the sheet) protects credibility in a health-related niche. Human approval is mandatory before anything is posted. Estimated time per carousel after setup: 10–15 minutes.
